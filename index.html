FUTURISTIC VISION

For Professionals
1

Addressing various issues faced by members at the right forum and at right time

  • Proper & periodical representation to the concerned authorities
  • Setting up of regular meetings between the authorities and the members
2

Strengthening Dispute Redressal Mechanism

  • Creating a more accessible, effective and time-bound dispute redressal mechanism for the members.
3

One CCGRT for every State

  • To launch CCGRT in all the States of SIRC
  • For Tamil Nadu, will take steps to open CCGRT in Chennai / Coimbatore
4

Promoting Employment Opportunities beyond metro cities

  • Bridging the gap between industry and professionals to create more opportunities for professionals in Tier-II & Tier-III cities
  • Take steps to induce a greater number of members into the CSBF with the aim to create a corpus of Rs.2 Crores
5

Insurance Coverage

  • Creating more awareness and enrolling maximum number of members to the New India Assurance ICSI for the large benefit of the members
6

ONE CAP-ONETREE

  • Will take steps to plant One Tree in every Career Awareness Programs conducted by all the Chapters falling under SIRC as an initiative under ESG.
7

POSH Training for Corporates

  • To reach out to Corporates for conducting POSH training through SIRC & other Chapters by engaging professionals and creating new opportunities.
8

CSR Awareness

  • Creating CSR awareness to the corporates by conducting CSR camp under SIRC
9

MSME & Start up Yatra

  • Entering into arrangements between MSME forum, Start up councils and creating of interaction with industry members
  • Focus on CS for being a member in advisory board of all MSME and Start up councils coming under State Governments and Central Government.

Other focuses

  • AI Tools for Practising CS
